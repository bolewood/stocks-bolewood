# Pre-IPO Anthropic & OpenAI Exposure — Open Data

Curated, sourced data on the public securities that hold equity or equity-equivalent
exposure to **Anthropic** and **OpenAI**, plus a documented method for converting each
holding into dollars of look-through exposure per **$100 of wrapper value**.

**Live calculator:** https://stocks.bolewood.com/ai

This repository is the **data and the method**. The calculator is one consumer of it; you
can reproduce every published figure without running the site.

---

## Why this exists

Public write-ups routinely call several different things "a stake." Disclosed ownership, a
capital commitment with no ownership figure, a fund's filed fair value divided by the round
that marked it, and an analyst's estimate are not the same claim and do not deserve the same
confidence — but they are usually printed in the same format.

Every record here is **typed by the kind of evidence behind it**, carries its own as-of date,
and cites a source. Where no primary source supports an ownership percentage, no percentage
is published.

## The Basis taxonomy

`basis` records how a figure is known. It is a discriminated union: each value carries only
the fields that actually exist for it, and any percentage that can be derived **is** derived
rather than stored.

| Basis | Meaning | Source class |
|---|---|---|
| `disclosed` | Issuer or investor disclosed an ownership percentage | Primary |
| `pro-forma` | Disclosed percentage **upon completion** of a pending transaction | Primary |
| `historical` | Disclosed at an earlier date; may since have been diluted | Primary |
| `filed-fv-equiv` | Fund's reported fair value ÷ the valuation that marked it | Primary |
| `carrying-value-equiv` | Corporate carrying value ÷ the valuation that marked it | Primary |
| `round-implied` | Investment amount ÷ round post-money | Primary |
| `commitment` | Capital promised; ownership and terms unavailable — **no percentage published** | Primary or secondary |
| `estimate` | Third-party estimate, not issuer disclosure | Secondary |

A single security can carry different bases for its two legs. Microsoft's OpenAI position is
`disclosed`; its Anthropic position is a `commitment` and therefore publishes no percentage
and no per-$100 figure.

## Denominators

"Wrapper value" is not always market capitalisation, and using the wrong denominator is an
easy way to get a badly wrong answer:

| `denominatorType` | Used for |
|---|---|
| `market-cap` | Listed operating companies and listed closed-end funds |
| `adr-equivalent-market-cap` | ADRs — issuer-equivalent shares, ratio sourced |
| `total-net-assets` | Unlisted / interval funds, taken from the same filing as the holdings |

---

## Filed vs. Estimated

Two coherent bases, never mixed:

- **Filed Only** — every input as of its filing date. Fully sourced, and stale by
  construction.
- **Estimated** — share counts, net assets and marks rolled forward *together*. For funds
  with active at-the-market programs, share issuance is modelled by a documented bridge.
  Clearly labelled as not company-reported.

Where a fund raised capital after its last filing and the deployment is unknown, the result
is published as a **range** rather than a point estimate: an upper bound assuming proceeds
bought more of the same holdings, a lower bound assuming they sit in cash.

---

## What is not modelled

Taxes, fees, carry, liquidation preferences, conversion terms, anti-dilution, ownership
caps, transfer restrictions, lockups, future financing, and differences between a fund's
mark and a primary round.

Specific exclusions are documented in `data/METHODOLOGY.md` — for example, DXYZ's OpenAI
profit participation units are not equity per the N-CSR and are excluded from IPO scaling.

These figures are **gross scenario estimates**, not NAV, liquidation value, expected
proceeds, or price targets.

## Corrections

Corrections are welcome and are the main reason this data is public.

Records typed `disclosed`, `pro-forma`, `historical`, `filed-fv-equiv`,
`carrying-value-equiv` or `round-implied` require a **primary source** — SEC EDGAR, issuer
IR, a depositary agreement. Secondary sources are accepted only for `estimate`, and for
`commitment` where no primary announcement exists.

Any pull request changing a number must update its value, its as-of date and its source
together. See `data/CONTRIBUTING.md`.

---

## Versioning and citation

`data/meta.json` carries `schemaVersion` and `methodologyVersion`. A figure moving because
an issuer filed a new report is categorically different from a figure moving because the
method changed, and `data/CHANGELOG.md` types every entry accordingly.

Cite a dated tag rather than `main`, so the numbers behind your reference don't move. See
`CITATION.cff`.
